## How to read results
- **CONFIRMED PUBLIC DOCUMENTATION:** an OEM manual, contract, policy or dated official product statement supports the *specific* claim. This does not imply independent physical testing.
- **VENDOR CLAIM:** marketing/seller statement without independently verified operational performance.
- **PARTIAL:** one component works locally, but full offline lifecycle/UK warranty is not established.
- **UNKNOWN:** insufficient evidence. This is not a proven failure.
- **NOT A FIT:** form factor (e.g., wheeled torso without legs) does not meet a full biped/ordinary chair sit–rise criterion.

## Common screening gates
1. Human-friendly complete-body morphology, documented household-safe movement and rescue.
2. UK repair capability: *which module*, by *which authorized entity*, at *which location*, with *which spare stock/SLA*.
3. User-controlled memory and media; service diagnostics without involuntary exposure of household recordings or keys.
4. Independent boot, basic motion/stop, diagnostics, license renewal and recovery over a disconnected WAN — **separately** verified.
5. UK private-home sale/delivery, suitable compliance evidence, insurance and full landed/support costs.
6. Local interfaces for audio/vision and a **robot-local safety controller**; off-board LLM/PC must never be a sole emergency-stop path.
