# UK repair, private data and offline-lifecycle diligence
**Public research snapshot: 2026-11-16**. Public evidence only; this is **not** a supplier audit, warranty determination or legal opinion. All decisions require model/version-specific written terms.

## Most important distinctions
- **Distributor / local demonstration ≠ OEM-authorized core-module replacement ≠ sealed actuator/BMS teardown.**
- **ROS 2 / SDK or local voice processing ≠ lifetime offline usability.** Verify initial activation, boot, low-level motion, emergency stop, diagnosis, calibration, signed firmware recovery, licensing renewal separately.
- **User-owned memory on a separate PC ≠ zero supplier access to cameras, microphones, device logs, or factory diagnostics.**
- **No published evidence ≠ proof that a repair option is nonexistent.**

## Common written question set (answer by model/firmware and country)
1. UK buyer contracting **legal entity** and warranty obligor. Which named joints, batteries/BMS, controllers and cameras can be inspected, swapped as FRUs, opened for teardown, or must be sent overseas? Who holds OEM authorization? Can a non-exclusive local engineer service externally replaceable modules without voiding remaining warranty?
2. Exact physical **UK spares** for specified SKU, order minimum, shipping origin (UK / EU / Asia / US), dispatch and end-user delivery days, temporary loaner, on-site technician SLA and cost.
3. With WAN unplugged from unboxing: first activation/calibration, boot, local mobility, hardware E-stop, diagnostics/log export, OS/firmware reinstallation, license renewals. Can signed recovery images be held locally and used without reaching manufacturer servers?
4. Does warranty/RMA require remote session, full camera/video, original spatial map, mic recording, user identity/memory, secret store or encrypted diagnostic file? Can customer storage be removed, sensitive streams physically isolated and only approved redacted codes transmitted? Specify roles, region, retention and written terms.
5. For home: documented threshold millimeters, floor traction, door crossing vs opening, ordinary-chair autonomous sit/rise success rate and safely disabled-robot relocation (brake/torque state).
6. Following component replacement or software change, is *independent third-party traceable functional/perception/data-egress testing* permitted? Do NOT assume such reports satisfy OEM warranty or are legally mandatory.

**Reply interpretation:** seller claims are not OEM-authorized technical permissions. Quote document title/version, date, SKU and evidence; assign VERIFIED DOCUMENT / VENDOR CLAIM / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN. Ask follow-up only on genuine gaps; separate repair rights from warranty claim approval.

## Specific findings and corrections
- **Autodiscovery** EU sales terms identify a German contracting entity, but their scope excludes private non-commercial individuals. An EU legal notice has cited a differently named legal entity. **Neither alone establishes the contracting party for a UK consumer**. Sources: https://autodiscovery.eu/policies/terms-of-service ; https://autodiscovery.eu/en/policies/legal-notice ; UK service claims: https://www.autodiscovery.co.uk/skills.html .
- **Reachy 2** local control/physical stop is partly documented; claims of guaranteed offline USB restoration should be **UNKNOWN** without a Reachy 2-specific full reimage procedure and binary/firmware availability. A manufacturer unboxing guide describes ~50kg and multi-person handling; it does not show a one-person emergency recovery procedure. Sources: https://docs.pollen-robotics.com/getting-started/setup-reachy2/unbox-reachy2/ ; https://docs.pollen-robotics.com/getting-started/setup-reachy2/stop-reachy2/ ; https://docs.pollen-robotics.com/getting-started/update-reachy2/update-software/ .
- **Engineered Arts** official Tritium descriptions distinguish robot-local OS and cloud AI; air-gapped firmware, support/license and hardware warranty are not publicly established: https://engineeredarts.com/blog/software/tritium .
- **Spot as a comparison, not a human-chair candidate:** OEM literature describes offline capability but encrypted service logs, showing that offline mobility and user-auditable diagnostics are independent properties: https://support.bostondynamics.com/ .
- **UK electronics-repair firms** may be able to repair generic drives/control boards and support independent tests. This is not a substitute for manufacturer-approved actuator calibration, signed controller firmware or BMS teardown.

## UK legal/logistics baseline — not legal advice
- **PUWER** is a work-equipment framework and should not automatically be applied to a purely private household. In a commercial/workplace test, it may be relevant. https://www.hse.gov.uk/work-equipment-machinery/puwer-overview.htm
- **GB CE/UKCA:** UK government accepts qualifying **CE or UKCA** for relevant machinery. No blanket standalone "UKCA certification for domestic robots" should be invented; check applicable machinery, EMC, electrical/battery and intended-use regimes. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/supply-of-machinery-safety-regulations-2008/supply-of-machinery-safety-regulations-2008-great-britain ; https://www.gov.uk/guidance/placing-ukca-or-ce-marked-products-on-the-market-in-great-britain
- **Consumer Rights Act 2015:** applicability follows the actual buyer (individual acting outside business/trade), trader and transaction; research/industrial marketing does not automatically negate consumer rights, nor does it confer unrestricted teardown privileges. https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/section/2 ; https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/section/23
- **UK GDPR & overseas remote access:** suppliers may be independent controllers/processors; granting an overseas separate organization remote access to personal data may constitute a restricted transfer. Whether UK GDPR applies and which transfer rules are triggered depend on actual facts, roles, location, and contracts; household use does not excuse separate supplier processing. https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/a-guide-to-international-transfers/are-we-making-a-restricted-transfer/
- **Return to overseas factory:** HMRC outward-processing relief can apply subject to conditions, never automatically. Include carrier, lithium battery shipment, RMA documents, customs agent, VAT and round-trip turnaround before assuming local support is cheap. https://www.gov.uk/guidance/using-outward-processing-to-process-or-repair-your-goods
