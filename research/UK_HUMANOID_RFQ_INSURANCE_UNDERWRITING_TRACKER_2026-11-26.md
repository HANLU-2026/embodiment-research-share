## 4. Quote acceptance checklist (mark UNKNOWN unless supplier replies)

For each **exact model/SKU and firmware**, capture:
1. **Named seller and importer**, offer reference/date/validity, VAT invoice and whether personal ownership is eligible, country of origin and **UK customs commodity code agreed by broker**.
2. Equipment **unit price + accessories + UK VAT + battery-certified packing + international freight + UK brokerage/duty + residential last-mile delivery + installation**. Incoterm (DDP/DAP/UK sale) and liability for damage in transit.
3. **Replacement battery** in UK and defective-battery return transport plus spare soles, skins and actuator parts, UK technician response and future support fees.
4. Required **supported local audio/high-level interfaces**, offline activation and security support, physical stop, power-loss fall handling, single-adult recovery. **Do not trade a P0 safety gate for a lower price.**
5. Insurance **acceptance, actual annual premium, insured value, liability cap, excess and restrictions**, rented dwelling status, pets/child presence, incident rescue/SLA.
6. Explicit **NOT QUOTED / NOT AVAILABLE / NOT SUPPORTED** separately from no response or publicly unknown.

## 5. Post-response cost formula (do NOT fill with speculative final quotes)
- UK vendor: `equipment + accessories + services + VAT shown on invoice + UK shipping/commissioning + annual insurance + first-year mandated support`, without duplicate import VAT.
- Non-UK vendor: `equipment converted at actual transaction/UK customs rate + true duty + import VAT on appropriate customs/VAT basis + freight/insurance/clearance + installation + annual insurance + support`; be explicit which items are tax-inclusive.
- Keep quotes in their *original currencies and dated FX basis*. Separate **cash-to-home** from **annual recurring software/service/insurance** and **potential emergency recovery**. If an insurer will not write the risk, do not assign a made-up premium.
